Contest Details

Develop your best UI Title/Menu for this month's Jam and compete to win big! Entries are judged by contestants and the host, @api.

You may create a new project or add on to any existing project so long as it's specified in your submission.

💸 Community Prize Pool

Any tips this project receives will boost the prize pool! Half of every tip will be shared equally among ALL contestants at the end of the contest.

How To Enter

To enter the Jam, simply post a comment on this project containing a link to your Websim creation. Your entry will automatically appear below.

What to Build?

The goal is to make a functional, clean UI—either a Title Screen or a Menu of some sort. The project MAY be more than just a menu (e.g., a functional game or application), but the focus is on UI. We will judge based on UI aesthetic and functionality, not gameplay or application experience. You may submit a project that you have already started so long as you specify in your entry.
